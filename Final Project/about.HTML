I do not have a dream because I will make it a reality

I am a designer and illustrator based in Brooklyn, NY from Honduras. I have a passion for creating unique and engaging visual content that tells a story.
With a background in graphic design and a love for digital art, I specialize in creating custom illustrations, character designs, and comic panels.
My work is inspired by my love for pop culture, fantasy, and science fiction, and I strive to bring my clients' visions to life through my art.

My ultimate goal is to create a significant impact on the world through my work.
